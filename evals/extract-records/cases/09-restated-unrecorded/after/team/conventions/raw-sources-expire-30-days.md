# Raw sources expire after 30 days

> [10:02] lead-example: Yes, still. And as before, raw sources expire after 30 days; only the records extracted from them persist.

## Rule

A raw source is deleted 30 days after capture. Records extracted from it persist.

## Why

No record held this rule, so restating it was the only place it was written down.
