# On-call handoff moves to Monday at ten

> 00:04:32 Ana Reyes: Okay, so, to be clear, because I said the other thing a minute ago: decided, on-call handoff is Monday at ten, after standup, starting next Monday the 16th. Friday is off the table. Note-writing stays with the outgoing person like always.

## Context

The 09:30 Monday handoff collided with standup, so handoff notes were thin.

## Options considered

Friday at 16:00 was decided first, then reversed in the same meeting: a Friday handoff leaves the incoming person
the weekend with nobody to ask, and the support desk is off on Friday afternoons.

## Decision

On-call handoff is Monday at 10:00, after standup, from Monday 2026-11-16.
