- `codify-lesson` skill (KAN-470): takes one open Lesson and proposes exactly one outcome. It may be a Convention
  that cites the lesson, as a records PR; the convention is active only under an accepted decision that covers the
  rule, else a draft, a superseding one too. It may be a change to a shared skill, hook or guide fragment, as a harness PR that cites the
  lesson and asks the owner for review, or stays a draft labelled `needs-owner-review` when
  the owner is its author. Or it is "not codifiable", with the reason written to the lesson. It drafts
  the follow-up that marks the lesson codified, never merges, and never codifies across a customer boundary or
  relaxes a guard. Six example lessons are in `tests/fixtures/codify-lesson/`.
